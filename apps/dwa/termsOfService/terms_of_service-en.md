### 2. Description of the Service

DWA is a sobriety tracking and personal progress application designed to help users monitor and celebrate their progress in reducing or avoiding alcohol consumption.

The App allows users to record their progress, track alcohol-free days, monitor personal records, and stay motivated throughout their journey.

Its main features include:

- Counting the number of days since the user's last alcohol consumption.
- Tracking the longest period without alcohol ever recorded.
- Maintaining a history of personal progress.
- Accessing a Hall of Fame to recognize personal achievements.
- Unlocking levels and trophies as motivational milestones.
- Home screen widgets to keep track of progress directly from the device.
- Additional functionality available through the PRO package.

DWA is intended to be a personal tracking and motivational tool. It does not provide medical treatment, diagnosis, or professional addiction counseling.
