### 1. About MTL

MTL (My Timeline) is a mobile application that allows you to organize and track events through timelines.

The application helps you record past events, monitor your progress, and create future events with notifications to remind you when they occur.

Its main features include:

- **Past Events:** Record events from your daily life and keep track of when things happened.
- **Future Events:** Schedule future events and receive notifications when they are due.
- **Multiple Timelines:** Organize events into separate projects or categories, creating dedicated timelines for different subjects.
- **Project Management:** Create and organize multiple projects to keep your timelines structured.
- **Backup and Restore:** Back up and restore your projects.
- **Dark Mode:** Use the application with a dark visual theme.

MTL is offered as a free application, with an optional PRO package that unlocks additional features.

The application may evolve over time, and new features may be introduced through future updates.
