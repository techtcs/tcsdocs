### 1. About TTH

TTH (Outliers: 10k Hours Counter) is a mobile application designed to help you record, track, and manage the time you dedicate to personal goals, activities, and skills.

The application is based on the concept of tracking 10,000 hours of practice and dedication as a way to monitor long-term progress toward mastery of a chosen activity.

Its main features include:

- **Activity Tracking:** Start and pause a timer to record the time dedicated to an activity.
- **History:** Keep a record of your completed sessions and accumulated dedication time.
- **Goals and Progress:** Track your progress toward your personal goals.
- **Levels and Trophies:** Earn levels and trophies as you accumulate practice time.
- **Motivational Notifications:** Receive notifications to encourage consistency and dedication.
- **Daily Progress Reports:** Follow your daily progress through reports.
- **Widgets:** Monitor your progress through device widgets.

Many features are available for free. An optional PRO package unlocks additional functionality, including:

- Dark mode.
- Creation and management of multiple goals.
- Tracking more than one goal simultaneously.
- Manual entry of dedicated hours without using the timer.
- Backup and restore of application data.
- Starting or pausing goals through a dedicated widget.

The application may evolve over time, and new features may be introduced through future updates.

### 2. The 10,000-Hour Concept

TTH uses the 10,000-hour concept as a motivational and progress-tracking framework.

The concept is commonly associated with the book *Outliers* by Malcolm Gladwell. However, the 10,000-hour figure should not be interpreted as a scientific guarantee or universal requirement for achieving expertise.

Individual results depend on many factors, including the nature of the activity, quality of practice, individual circumstances, prior experience, and other variables.

TTH is a tracking and organizational tool. It does not guarantee expertise, professional success, or any particular result from the time recorded in the application.
